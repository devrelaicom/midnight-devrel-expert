Maintainer pattern

A "contribution steward" is absorbing community PRs

@oduameh ran the same move at least four times this fortnight: take a community draft, apply every piece of review feedback, complete the frontmatter, credit the original author, and open a clean superseding PR. #1131 superseded #980, #1132 superseded #1102, #1130 superseded #1042, and #1133 reworked #1109. It's why closed-unmerged is high but morale-safe — contributors keep their credit and their content ships.

Style enforcement

There is an explicit, consistently-applied docs style guide

The same corrections recur in every review: remove em-dashes, kill passive voice, split sentences over ~35 words, standardize "DApp" capitalization, and hold page descriptions to 120–160 characters. #1073 was flagged for a 92-char description; #1126 got a full "style fixes" block. Reviews are specific and constructive — a strong signal for anyone contributing.
